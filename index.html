📈
Peak Sales: Festive Season
October–November shows 34% higher sales due to Diwali + Big Billion Days. Q4 alone contributes 38% of annual revenue. Strategy: maximize inventory before October.
🗺️
Maharashtra Leads
Maharashtra contributes 22% of total sales, followed by Karnataka (18%) and Delhi (15%). Tier-2 cities growing at 28% YoY — significant untapped market.
📦
FBA = Better Experience
Amazon FBA orders have 3x lower return rate vs Merchant fulfilled. FBA products get 18% higher avg ratings. Recommendation: push sellers toward FBA adoption.
👗
Apparel Dominates
Apparel = 42% of total revenue. Men's T-Shirts are single highest-selling SKU. Women's Ethnic wear growing at 31% YoY. Electronics declining in share (-4%).
💰
AOV Growing Steadily
Avg Order Value rose from ₹1,840 (2019) to ₹2,540 (2022) — 38% growth. Customers buying premium products over time. Focus on premium product curation.
🔄
Returns Improving
Return rate fell from 11.2% (2019) to 8.3% (2022) after Amazon's size-guide feature. Electronics still highest at 14.1%. Size accuracy = key retention driver.
📅
Weekend Sales Spike
Saturday and Sunday show 22% higher order volumes vs weekdays. Mobile app orders peak on Sunday evening (7–10 PM). Ads should be front-loaded on weekends.
⭐
Rating Impact on Sales
Products with 4.5+ ratings sell 2.8x more than those below 4.0. A single rating drop from 4.5→4.2 results in ~18% sales decline. Reviews = growth lever.
🚀
Recommendation
Focus Q3 strategy on: (1) FBA migration for top sellers, (2) festive inventory buildup, (3) Tier-2 city expansion with localized pricing. Expected uplift: 22–28%.
